## ⚡ What is SportsAgentic?

Sports never stop, and neither should your info. SportsAgentic builds **AI agents that follow the games for you**. Skip the ten tabs, the refreshing, and the hunt for who's hurt. Ask a question and get the answer.

> *"Who's on the injured list tonight?"*
> *"What's on the schedule this weekend?"*
> *"Did my team make a move?"*
